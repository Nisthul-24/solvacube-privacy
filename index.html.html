SolvaCube Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

SolvaCube ("the app") helps you solve twisty puzzles (3x3, 2x2, 4x4 and Pyraminx) step by step. This policy explains what information the app uses. It is provided by MiniQ Studios ("we", "us").

Summary

  • We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • The colours you enter, and the solutions the app computes, are processed and stored on your device only.
  • The camera scan reads cube colours on your device. Photos are not saved and are not sent anywhere.
  • The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID.

Information processed on your device

The puzzle colours you enter or scan, your settings (such as animation speed and colour-blind letters), and your progress in an unfinished solve are stored locally on your device so the app can work offline and let you continue later. We do not receive this data.

Camera

If you choose "Scan", the app asks for permission to use the camera. Images are analysed on your device to detect sticker colours. Images and video are not stored, not uploaded and not shared. You can deny the permission and enter colours manually instead.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads using Google AdMob. AdMob and Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions) the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Manage Ad Consent. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and the Google Privacy Policy.

Data we do not collect or sell

We do not sell your personal data. We do not collect your location, contacts, files, microphone audio or photos.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

Data retention and deletion

Because app data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Data held by Google AdMob is governed by Google's policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available at this address, with the date at the top.
